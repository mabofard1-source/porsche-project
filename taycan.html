Porsche High-Voltage battery guarantee.

Every new, all-electric Porsche is delivered with a worldwide guarantee against material and manufacturing defects on the high-voltage (HV) battery, which is valid for eight years or up to 160,000 km/100,000 miles.

In addition, we guarantee a minimum residual capacity of the high-voltage battery throughout the warranty period¹.

¹80% of the original net battery capacity is guaranteed for up to three years or 60,000 km/38,000 miles, after which Porsche guarantee at least 70% residual capacity until the end of the HV battery guarantee.
