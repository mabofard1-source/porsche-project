Porsche HV battery warranty.

Every fully-electric Porsche is delivered with a worldwide high-voltage battery warranty covering material and manufacturing defects, which is valid for eight years or up to a mileage of 160,000 km.¹

We also guarantee the remaining capacity of the high-voltage battery. 80% of the original net battery capacity is guaranteed for up to three years or 60,000 km¹, after which we guarantee at least 70% remaining capacity until the end of the HV battery warranty.

¹ Whichever comes first.

Conquer new frontiers. On-road and off-road.

Thanks to adaptive air suspension including Porsche Active Suspension Management, the powerful 300 kW (408 PS) Cayenne Coupé delivers a comfortable and dynamic driving experience on any terrain.

Adaptive air suspension.

The air spring struts allow a great balance between comfort and sportiness. The 2-valve system ensures a constant vehicle position and the height adjustment ensures variable ground clearance.

Rear-axle steering.¹

Improves performance and day-to-day usability. At low speeds, the turning circle is reduced, agility is increased and parking becomes noticeably easier. At higher speeds, driving stability is increased.

Being responsible.

Electric performance. Expansion of renewable energies.

Porsche is supporting the development of wind and solar energy plants to cover the expected electricity demand of the Cayenne Electric fleet based on a driving distance of 200,000 km at certified consumption.

Resource-conserving production and supply chain.

We are constantly working to reduce the environmental footprint of our vehicles across their entire lifecycle, prioritizing sustainable materials and clean energy.

Resource-conserving production and supply chain.

Porsche tests the Cayenne Electric's energy storage system for long service life and offers a warranty against material and manufacturing defects. A globally available high-voltage battery repair concept, along with an estimated 90% recyclability, is intended to further support resource conservation.

High-voltage battery recycling.

Raw materials can be recovered at the end of a battery's useful life to support a closed-loop circular economy for future electric vehicle components.
